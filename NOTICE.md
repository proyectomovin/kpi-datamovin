# Notices

DataMovin KPI is a modified distribution of KoboToolbox KPI and includes
KoboCAT and OpenRosa-compatible components.

The upstream project is available at:

- https://github.com/kobotoolbox/kpi
- https://github.com/kobotoolbox/kobocat

The source baseline is KoboToolbox KPI release `2.026.21a`. DataMovin's visual
identity, operational defaults and deployment metadata are modifications made
by Movin.
